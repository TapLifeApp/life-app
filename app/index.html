What does research say?

For adults, public-health guidance recommends working toward 150–300 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity per week (or an equivalent mix), plus muscle-strengthening activity on at least 2 days per week. People who are inactive should start with small amounts and gradually increase frequency, intensity and duration.

This is a longer-term health guideline, not a pass/fail test or a requirement to reach immediately. The right starting point depends on your current activity, abilities, health and circumstances.

Source: World Health Organization guidelines ↗

How we decide your next step

  1. Begin with a doable action.
  2. Track what you actually completed.
  3. Check how manageable it felt.
  4. Repeat or adjust before increasing difficulty.
The prototype does not yet evaluate exercise form, medical conditions or readiness automatically. It should never increase difficulty just to keep a streak alive.

Evidence note: public-health recommendations describe population-level targets. Individualized exercise prescriptions and medical advice require more context and, where appropriate, a qualified professional.

Privacy and data

This is an early prototype. Your preferences and workout history stay in local browser storage on this device. There is no account, cloud sync, or production-grade data protection yet.

Export creates a JSON backup. Restore replaces the current preferences and history after validation and confirmation. Backup files may contain personal information; store them somewhere private.
